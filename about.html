About Us

We're Arthanta

A small team based in Kalaburagi, building digital products for ambitious companies. No corporate BS. Just good work.

Started in a college lab. Still building.

Arthanta began in 2025 when three friends realized that most tech agencies were either too expensive for startups or too low-quality for serious products. There had to be a middle ground.

So we built one. We're still early — a small team working on real problems, learning every day, and shipping projects we're genuinely proud of.

What hasn't changed: we still keep our team small, our communication direct, and our standards high. No account managers. No endless meetings. Just people who build things.

The way we work

You talk to builders

Full visibility

Fixed pricing

Iterative delivery

What We Believe

Principles that guide us

Not plaque-on-the-wall values. These are the rules we actually follow every day.

Ship first, polish later

Working software in production beats perfect mockups in Figma. We iterate fast based on real feedback, not assumptions.

No black boxes

You'll know exactly what we're building, why we're building it that way, and how much it costs. No surprises, no excuses.

Quality is non-negotiable

We'd rather say no to a project than deliver something mediocre. Every product we ship has our name on it.

Build for users, not stakeholders

The best products solve real problems for real people. Everything else — features, timelines, budgets — follows from that.

Speed is a feature

We move fast without cutting corners. Clear communication, flat decision-making, and a bias toward action.

Invest in relationships

Most of our clients come back for more work or refer us. That's the metric that matters most to us.

Want to work with us?

No pitch decks. No sales calls. Just tell us about your project and we'll tell you if we're a fit.
